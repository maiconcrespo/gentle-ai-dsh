## Next developer guide

### Start here

1. Update `main` and create a new branch for your sub-issue. Do not reuse `feat/3-v2`; it is merged.
2. Read the sub-issue (#10 or #11) and its specs: S3 and S8 for B, S5 and S9 for C.
3. Run `npm test`. New test files must be imported by `test/index.js`.
4. When done, record the commit and evidence here (Tasks and Log), open a PR that closes your sub-issue, and get the maintainer's approval before merging.
