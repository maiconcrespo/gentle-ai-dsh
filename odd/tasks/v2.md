### Parallel contribution contract

- A and B are **development units**, not fully independent products: the ODD
  prompt depends on actual role/tool availability for end-to-end behavior.
  B may develop from `main` while A is reviewed; C waits for both.
- Use separate branches/worktrees for concurrent work; never have two writers
  changing `feat/3-v2` at once. Agree on the exact exported role/tool names
  before integrating the orchestrator with the presets.
- Keep role work in new files where possible. `index.js`,
  `scripts/sync-gentle-ai.mjs`, `package.json` and `test/index.js` are shared
  integration points: one integrator owns those edits at a time. Exchange a
  small integration patch rather than claiming disjoint ownership there.
- Prefer B based on `main` with its own role-sync helper/tests. If B needs A's
  APIs or shared-file edits, declare that dependency explicitly and make its
  PR stacked on A instead; do not present it as independently mergeable.
- Keep tests and docs for each behavior in its delivery unit. A's generated
  prompt accounts for much of its 568-line candidate; do not add B to it.
- Do not re-run sync without inspecting its writes: it regenerates persona,
  skills and attribution as well as the orchestrator. Preserve unrelated work.
